How do I download my file from Etsy?

  1. After paying, Etsy sends you a confirmation e-mail.
  2. Sign in to Etsy, open You → Purchases and reviews.
  3. Next to the order click Download files.

Menu names can change; see Etsy's own help if yours looks different.

How do I open the PDF and the spreadsheet?

PDF: any reader (Adobe Reader, your browser, Preview). Spreadsheet: Excel, Google Sheets (File → Import) or LibreOffice.

Something does not work

Write to with your order number and what you see. We reply quickly.

Refunds and cancellations

Purchases are made on Etsy, so Etsy handles payment, invoices and returns under its own policies. Digital items are generally not refundable after download; contact us first and we will try to fix it.
