Help & FAQ

How do I download my file from Etsy?

  1. After paying, Etsy sends you a confirmation e-mail.
  2. Sign in to Etsy and open You → Purchases and reviews.
  3. Next to your order click Download files.

Menu names on Etsy can change. If yours looks different, see Etsy's own help for digital downloads.

How do I open the PDF and the spreadsheet?

PDF: any reader (Adobe Reader, your browser, Preview). Spreadsheet: Excel, Google Sheets (File → Import) or LibreOffice.

Something does not work

Write to our contact page with your Etsy order number and what you see.

Refunds and returns

Purchases are made on Etsy, so Etsy handles payment, invoices and returns under its own policies. If something is wrong with a file, contact us first and we will fix it.

Our products are created with the help of AI and reviewed by a human before release. Purchases are handled by Etsy. Prices in EUR; Etsy may show them in your currency.
